## 1. The Initial Setup (What You Sold vs. Bought)

### A. The Put Side (4 Lots = 100 Shares)

* **Sold 24,050 PE** at ₹33.00 $\rightarrow$ You collected: $100 \times 33 = \mathbf{+₹3,300}$ cash inflow.
* **Bought 23,950 PE** at ₹15.85 $\rightarrow$ You spent: $50 \times 15.85 = \mathbf{-₹792.50}$ cash outflow *(Note: Deployed as 2 lots protection)*.
* **Net Starting Cash Value (Put Side):** $+3,300 - 792.50 = \mathbf{+₹2,507.50}$ credit collected.

### B. The Call Side (2 Lots = 50 Shares)

* **Sold 24,450 CE** at ₹14.90 $\rightarrow$ You collected: $50 \times 14.90 = \mathbf{+₹745}$ cash inflow.
* **Bought 24,550 CE** at ₹6.40 $\rightarrow$ You spent: $50 \times 6.40 = \mathbf{-₹320}$ cash outflow.
* **Net Starting Cash Value (Call Side):** $+745 - 320 = \mathbf{+₹425}$ credit collected.

$$\text{Total Gross Cash Collected at Open} = ₹2,507.50 + ₹425 = \mathbf{+₹2,932.50}$$

## 2. The Current Valuation (What it Costs to Buy Back Now)

To close an option trade that you originally sold, you must perform the exact opposite action: **Buy back what you sold, and sell what you bought.**

Let's look at the current Last Traded Prices (**LTP**) on your screen right now:

### A. The Put Side Current Value

* *Cost to buy back:* $100 \times 18.50 = \mathbf{-₹1,850}$
