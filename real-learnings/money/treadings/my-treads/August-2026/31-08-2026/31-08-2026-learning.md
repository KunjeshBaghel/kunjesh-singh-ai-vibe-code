# 31-08-2026 — Learnings

## Trade: NIFTY 01-Sep Bear Call Spread 24,200/24,400 · 5 lots · Net +₹3,131 (0.52%)

---

### 1. Dhan MCP is fixed — new login flow

`mcp__dhan__authenticate` → browser URL. If browser shows `{"error":"invalid_client"}` → **call `authenticate` again** — it generates a fresh OAuth `client_id`. First call's client_id can be stale from a prior server session. The MCP now has agent-style tools post-auth (`market_data_agent_tool`, etc.). Verified with `expirylist` → NIFTY dates returned. **Always verify with `expirylist`, never `fundlimit`.**

### 2. Dhan field name: `UnderlyingSeg` not `UnderlyingSegment`

Using `UnderlyingSegment` returns `813: Invalid SecurityId` — a misleading error. The correct field is `UnderlyingSeg`. Index IDs: NIFTY=13, BANKNIFTY=25, SENSEX=51. Already documented in `dhan-rest-api-needs-two-headers.md`.

### 3. Kotak order sequence matters for NRML spreads

Placing SELL (short leg) before the LONG leg (hedge) triggers `RMS:Margin Exceeds` because the system sees a naked short. **Correct sequence: BUY the long leg first (it fills), then place the SELL — the hedge is in place and margin is recognised.** Rejected order does not count against you; only filled orders affect positions.

### 4. The sizing cap was the binding constraint, not the market

At 5 lots: ₹3,131 net. At 20 lots: same structure, same P&L ratio, ~₹12,500 net (2.08%). The stop (spread at 53.85 pts) was never remotely threatened — the short strike reached 179 pts OTM at exit. The old `Lots = risk cap ÷ loss per lot` formula prevented the better outcome. **New policy: size by conviction and available margin (60-70% cap); define stop in rupee terms; exit is manual and supervised. CLAUDE.md updated.**

### 5. §8.7.4 — OI wall unwind is a real-time signal

At 14:06, the 24,000 PE OI fell 10.8L in 10 minutes as NIFTY approached 24,000. This is the §8.7.4 signal: "floor being removed." It did not hurt our Bear Call Spread (our short was 179 pts away) but it is worth watching — a floor removal followed by a sharp bounce can be a dangerous whipsaw. **Always pull OI at every scheduled check, not just spot and VIX.**

### 6. 1-DTE expiry-eve is delta-driven, not theta-driven (intraday)

On expiry-EVE (today), the bulk of remaining premium expires tomorrow. Intraday P&L comes from delta (directional move), not theta. The market fell from 24,067 (entry) to 24,020 (exit) = 47 pts → 24,200 CE dropped from 45.35 to 32.40 = 12.95 pts. That's the delta effect. Pure theta in 2 hours would have only given 2-4 pts. **When entering on expiry-eve, have a directional thesis, not just a "theta harvest" thesis.**

### 7. The entry timing (dead zone) worked out this session

Entry was at 12:24 — 6 minutes inside the §8.8.1 dead zone (10:30-12:30). The market had already found its low (23,994 at 11:00) and was consolidating. The dead zone rule exists to avoid entering during volatile morning reversals — today's entry was justified by the stabilised range. But: **the dead zone rule should be respected in general; this was an acceptable exception because the range had clearly compressed for 1+ hour.**

### 8. "Deploy ₹6L" ≠ "risk ₹6L"

The available margin (₹4.89L free) and the deployed margin (₹2.13L for 5 lots) are collateral, not risk. The actual risk per the old framework was ₹5,835 at the stop. These are three completely different numbers and must be tracked separately. The confusion between "deploy ₹6L" and "risk cap ₹6,000" was the root of the undersizing.

### 9. PE-first principle — sell the expensive side (§8.5.4 added)

Expert feedback post-session: "CE selling is risky...PE selling is better." Validated as structurally true for neutral/sideways days. NIFTY has persistent put skew — OTM puts carry higher IV than equidistant calls → PE sellers systematically earn more per unit of delta risk.

**Rule (new §8.5.4 in strategy_ref_book.md):** For Sideways, Slightly Bullish, Strongly Bullish views → default to PE selling (Bull Put Spread, Jade Lizard, put-heavy condor). CE selling (Bear Call Spread) is appropriate ONLY when: (a) view is explicitly Slightly/Strongly Bearish, (b) overhead call OI wall is visible as resistance, (c) §8.5.2 grid points there.

Today's trade was a valid override — Slightly Bearish + compression break DOWN + 181.8L call wall at 24,200. The expert's principle applies correctly on flat days where selling calls without a bearish thesis = selling cheap premium on the wrong side.
