
## 16:03 — Rulebook review: "why is only a one-sided vertical permitted?"

**User challenge:** *"is this good? we should be open for all kind of structure if they make sense…
the data of the day decides what is good, we can not pre-decide to ignore certain type of structure."*

**Answer — the lock is a capacity constraint, not a market opinion.** Every locked structure fails an
operator-side test, and not one of them fails a market test:

**Where the challenge is right (2 defects, logged as carried-forward items 4 and 5):** Jade Lizard and

and 01-Sep are what that looks like from the inside. Lock **upheld**.
