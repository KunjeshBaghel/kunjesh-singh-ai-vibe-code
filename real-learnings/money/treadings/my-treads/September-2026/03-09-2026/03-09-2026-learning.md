## The one that matters — compare realised to implied *before* pricing strikes

- SENSEX realised **232.98 pts in 98 minutes (2.38 pts/min)**. The ATM-forward straddle priced
  **295.25 pts (≈1.40 pts/min) for the remaining 265 minutes.** Realised was running **≈1.7× implied.**
- **VRP was negative before friction.** That is the exact condition where selling premium is unpaid,
  and it explains why *every* strike failed at once instead of one filter tripping in isolation.
- **New habit: run this two-line ratio right after Gate 2, before touching strikes.** It is arithmetic
  on numbers already in hand — day range ÷ minutes elapsed vs straddle ÷ minutes remaining — and today
  it would have reached the verdict twenty minutes and one full chain analysis earlier.
- **A low VIX is not a cheap-vol signal, it is a low-*implied* signal.** VIX 11.10 read as "calm" all
  morning while the tape delivered a 233-pt range. Low IV with high RV is the worst tape for a seller,
  and it looks identical to the best one on a VIX quote alone.
