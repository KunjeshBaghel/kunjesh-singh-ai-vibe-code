**Findings.**

1. **Relaxing intraday-only does not pay on this chain.** 6 of 7 filter-passing structures are
   negative-EV even under the *generous* assumption of hold-to-expiry with zero exit slippage. The
   single positive one returns **₹74 against ₹7,710 of risk (1.0%)** — inside sampling noise.
2. **Sample caveat, stated plainly:** 249 *overlapping* 3-session windows ≈ **~83 independent** ones.
   A +1.0% edge is not distinguishable from zero at that sample size. This is not evidence *for*
   overnight holds being profitable; it is evidence they are not obviously profitable.
3. **Scope caveat:** this tests a **VIX 10.80** chain. It shows overnight holds do not pay *when
   there is no volatility risk premium*. It does **not** prove they never pay. The test must be
   repeated on a VIX 15+ day before any general conclusion is drawn.
4. **Gate 1 was not the binding constraint today.** At VIX 10.80 the options are not overpriced, so
   no credit structure has edge at *any* DTE. Removing Gate 1 would have bought more risk and no edge.
5. **Unexpected, and the most useful result: EV/maxL rises with c/W.** Everything below roughly
   **c/W 40%** is negative. The current floor is **15%** (TC §6 row 2, "20%+ preferred"), which admits
   the entire negative-EV band. The far-OTM "safer" spreads (c/W 22–30%) are the *worst* performers —
   they win more often (P(untouched) 66–73%) but the payoff ratio is far too thin to cover the losses.
