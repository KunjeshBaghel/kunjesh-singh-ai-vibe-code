### The rule change (11:22) — and what it cost me to get there

* **I defended Gate 1 twice before testing it properly, and it was wrong both times.** My first pass
  tested only "should we hold overnight at VIX 10.8" — a chain on which *everything* was negative-EV,
  so of course the answer was no. That is testing the change on the one day guaranteed to reject it.
  **The right test was the 2-D sweep: EV across VIX × holding period.** The moment I ran it the
  answer was obvious and it was the user's: holding period moves EV ~2 points, VIX moves it ~20, and
  the 5-session column is the *best* one at every VIX in every sample window. Gate 1 was excluding
  the best-performing column.
* **Lesson about my own reasoning: a filter that has never been measured is not conservative, it is
  just old.** I treated Gate 1's existence as evidence for it. It was a proxy for "theta must pay
  inside one session" — an argument that only held while the 2:30 flat held. Nobody had re-derived it
  after that.
* **Retracted from my own analysis the same session:** (1) "raise c/W floor to ~40%" — artefact of one
  degenerate chain; the real winners run c/W 20–25% and the 15% floor is right. (2) The VIX-20+ EV
  figures are model artefact — BS priced against a 13.2%-rv sample cannot represent a VIX-20 regime,
  so the HOSTILE ceiling stays. **Both were caught by re-testing my own claim, not by being careful
  the first time.**
* **How to loosen a rule without it being motivated reasoning** — now written into TC §15 as a
  four-condition carve-out. The one that does the real work: **the change must authorise no trade on
  the day it is made.** VIX was 10.80; the new rule blocks today exactly as the old one did. If a rule
  change would have let me take a trade the same afternoon, I should not trust it.
* **Removing a veto obliges you to add the safety back somewhere.** Net blocking power did not fall:
  new VIX floor · one *open* structure ever · SL re-placed every session · kill switch every session ·
  declared exit binding · two new §12 violations.
* **The honest cost, stated so it cannot be forgotten: an SL-Limit does not survive the night.** On a
  multi-session hold the ₹3,500 stop is market-hours-only and a gap converts it to the ₹10,500
  structural cap. Bounded, pre-sized (§3 set the structural cap = daily breaker for exactly this), but
  real. **Size multi-session holds as though ₹10,500 is the number you will get.**
* **Diagnosis to carry forward: 04-Sep was never a calendar problem.** Realised vol 13.17% vs VIX
  10.80 = **VRP −2.37 vol points.** There was no premium to sell. The correct no-trade code today is
  **`UNPAID`**, not `Too small` — and the 28-Aug no-trade was mis-coded the same way, which is why its
  real cause went unexamined for a week.

### The BANKNIFTY unlock (12:40) — and the lesson that came back the same day

* **I mis-framed the lock before I measured it.** I called §11's BANKNIFTY lock a *competence gate*,
  "categorically different from Gate 1's bad proxy." It isn't. A trade count in NIFTY/SENSEX has no
  causal link to BANKNIFTY's risk — it is a proxy too, just a more flattering one. **The universe is
  three instruments; banning one without measurement is a 33% cut to the opportunity set.** That is
  the second time today I defended a rule by its existence rather than its evidence.
* **What actually made the lock expensive was my own change three hours earlier.** Retiring Gate 1
  turned BANKNIFTY from a ~2-day-a-month instrument into an every-day one. **A rule's cost is not
  fixed — changing one rule reprices every rule downstream of it.** Nobody re-audits the cheap ones.
* ⚠️ **The far-OTM trap, and it caught me twice in one day.** The model offered BANKNIFTY spreads at
  5–6% OTM, **91–98% win rate, EV +33% of max loss**, and they survived a non-overlapping re-test.
  They are still untradeable: 251 sessions hold only **14 independent 17-session windows**, so that
  EV is a tail *asserted from 14 points*. The model-free number said the reverse — σ_ATM 11.41% vs
  realised 16.61/20.75/13.45%, **negative VRP on every window.**
  **Ruling now in TC §11a: when strike-level EV and model-free VRP disagree, VRP wins** — it doesn't
  need the tail estimated. This is verbatim the *"high win rate is not edge"* lesson from this
  morning, and it still fooled me for a whole analysis pass.
* **Longer tenor = fewer independent observations, and the trap gets worse, not better.** Weekly
  NIFTY gives ~50 independent windows a year; monthly BANKNIFTY gives 14. **The instruments that
  permit the longest holds are the ones I can measure least well.** Say the independent-window count
  out loud before quoting any EV on a monthly.
* **Removing a veto obliges you to notice the hole it was covering.** This morning's change permitted
  multi-session holds — but §10b's VRP test only had an *intraday* pts/min form. For an entire
  session the headline gate had **no defined form for the holds it had just authorised.** Now split
  into Form A / Form B. Loosening a rule leaves a shape behind; check what used to fill it.
* **A vol gate has to be measured on the thing being traded.** India VIX is NIFTY-derived. Applying
  it to BANKNIFTY would have been a number that looks like a measurement and isn't. BANKNIFTY needs
  its own `σ_ATM`; SENSEX uses India VIX as a **proxy, and must be logged as one.**
* **Two live bugs found only because the lock was challenged:** TC said BANKNIFTY's strike interval
  was **200 when it is 100** — silently banning width-100 BN spreads that exist — and Dhan returns
  **bid/ask 0.00 across the whole BN monthly chain**. **A locked instrument's constants never get
  checked**, so a lock quietly protects its own bad data. Worth re-reading any other locked row.
* **The unlock authorises no trade today** (σ_ATM 11.41% < 16 floor, VRP `UNPAID` on all windows) —
  the §15 carve-out test, passed for the second time today.
