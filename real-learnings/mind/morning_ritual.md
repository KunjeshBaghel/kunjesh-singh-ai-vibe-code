# Morning Ritual

> A daily guide for self-observation, physical and mental fitness, meaningful work, family, and growth.

## Self-observation — all areas

### Eating

- **Avoid sweets, including sweet tea.** Sweets (even healthier varieties) tend to increase cravings and worsen sinus issues.
- **Avoid cold items** such as refrigerated fruit, ice cream, and cold drinks; they can aggravate sinus issues.
- **Keep oil low, especially restaurant-fried food.** Chicken can be healthy, but heavily fried or spicy restaurant versions are best avoided or kept to a minimum.
  - Limit chips, namkeen, fried chicken, fries, and other oily snacks.
- **Prefer less calorie-dense food.** Keep dry fruits to one handful per day.
- **Track the basics.**
  - Drink about 4 litres of water daily.
  - Track food calories with MyFitnessPal.
  - Remember: gym performance depends heavily on diet.

### Behaviour and growth

- Build genuine strength in meaningful areas of life: career, sports, hobbies, cooking, fitness, swimming, and creative work.
- Ask: **What are my differentiators?** Work toward excellence across these five forts:

  | Fort | Focus | Current reflection |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | One | Money | Use the advantage of age; calculate and plan carefully. |
  | Two | Mental and physical health | Use natural advantages and make this unbeatable. |
  | Three | Knowledge | Regain office depth and protect professional reputation. |
  | Four | Fame / impact | Make a substantial improvement through valuable work. |
  | Five | Kindness and humility | Practice it deliberately; do not take yourself too seriously. |

  - Knowledge and health are tightly coupled.
  - Build respect through real work, not appearances alone.

- **Do not waste time.** Time is the most valuable resource.
  - Avoid social-media short videos unless they are clearly productive.
  - Do not overconsume general news feeds. Use focused finance and general-news sources instead.
  - You do not need to know everything; become excellent in a few chosen dimensions.
  - Too much disconnected information is mentally draining—consume with intention.

- **Manage time and switch modes thoughtfully.** Do not let one goal consume the others.
  - Fitness must not block professional growth; learning finance must not displace office work.
  - Try to make daily progress across the key paths below.

  | Priority | Path | Daily focus |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | P0 | Physical health | Gym, food, and water |
  | P0 | Mental health | Meditation and reading |
  | P0 | Family health | Insurance and care |
  | P0 | Money | Main profession and side streams |
  | P1 | Office work and reputation | Availability, proactive communication, delivery |

- **Use tools that improve productivity.**
  - Google Calendar for events and birthdays.
  - A paper day planner for scheduling tasks.
  - Smartwatch timers for focused sprints, sitting breaks, and walks.
  - MyFitnessPal for daily intake tracking.
  - A password manager and two-factor authentication wherever possible.

- **Be honest and grounded.**
  - Never lie to impress anyone. It is fine not to know something.
  - Accept areas where you are behind; work consistently to master the few areas that matter to you.

- **Protect the mind from parasites.**
  - Do not let anger, jealousy, or other harmful emotions consume attention and energy.
  - Keep your head down and focus on improvement; life is too short for comparison.

### Habits to practice

- Take deep breaths regularly; they bring calm and energy.
- Check whether the mind is helping your intentional focus.
- Be the observer. Do not attach to unhelpful thoughts; the mind is a double-edged sword.
- If not doing something useful, avoid screens. Protect your eyes and attention.
- In work-from-home life, keep the team updated, attend meetings and respond to Slack on time, be proactive, finish tasks, then learn from them.

## Physical fitness

- Practice pranayama.
- Practice yoga.
- Count steps: aim for **more than 25,000** daily.
- Drink more than **4 litres** of water; target 70% (about 2.8 litres) before the gym.
- Eat lighter at night and aim for four meals with roughly four-hour gaps.
  - Eat within defined windows rather than grazing continuously.
- Take appropriate boosters or supplements as needed.
- Go to the gym six days each week.

## Daily commitments

- Do not share knowledge unless a seeker asks. Speak less; do not brag.
- Do not hurt anyone. Speak kindly, unless someone is intentionally trying to put you down.
- Do not correct people unless it is truly necessary, especially in a gathering.
- Practise what you usually resist: release harmful emotions.
  - I will not be angry today.
  - I will not be jealous today.
- Practise gratitude. Self-care reduces stress; solving problems creates happiness. Be a problem solver.
- Nourish the body with clean, healthy food. Follow the eating order: **fibre → protein → carbs**.
- Make time to speak with loved ones. Take breaks between work and be social in your own way.
- Laugh often and loudly.
- Nurture creativity. When life is steady and the mind is clear, it will emerge naturally.
